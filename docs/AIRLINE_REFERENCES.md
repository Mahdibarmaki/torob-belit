# Airline identities and illustrative aircraft

Research checked on 2026-10-15 for the restored, entirely offline Flight AI demo. Airline names and aircraft model names are factual reference metadata. Flight numbers, routes, schedules, aircraft assignment, fares, seats, baggage, seller offers and cancellation outcomes in the authored demo are synthetic. These references do **not** establish a real flight, current operating fleet, current route, bookable inventory or airline performance.

## Review method and limitations

- Prefer airline, manufacturer and aviation-authority publications. Do not treat similarly named third-party `iata.codes`/`iata-codes.com` sites as IATA itself.
- Aircraft choices are a deliberately conservative allowlist. A real model name alone is insufficient evidence to associate it with a carrier. Models such as Boeing 737-800 and BAe 146 need not be included just because they appeared among the user's examples.
- Older authority tables are used only for historical associations. Search indexes expose the cited 2014/2015 ICAO tables, but the browser tool could not fetch those two full legacy PDFs during this review. This limitation is recorded rather than implying a current fleet audit. Direct manufacturer, Qeshm, Sepehran, FAA and mirrored AAIB sources were accessible.
- The Varesh source is original authority-authored report content on a third-party mirror. Its title page and aircraft/operator section were inspected. The primary publication host was not accessible; the mirror is not a new independent verification of authenticity or present fleet status.
- Company-controlled social profiles are first-party statements with weaker publication dating than manufacturer announcements. Use them only to corroborate model association. Airline homepages that were unavailable through the research tool were not bypassed.
- Catalog identities can use either a verified three-letter ICAO code or the explicitly tagged Y9 IATA code. Never imply those are the same code system. Unconfirmed IATA fields remain absent. A future provider adapter must use documented operating identity and code-system mappings rather than infer identity from this demo catalog.
- Do not import logos, livery photographs, aircraft registrations, real flight numbers, schedules, operational outcomes or fleet totals. No external assets or requests are required at runtime.

## Product wording

The shared label is «اطلاعات آزمایشی». Flight details should identify the aircraft as an illustrative demo assignment, for example «مدل هواپیما در این پرواز آزمایشی است؛ تخصیص واقعی تأیید نشده». Synthetic flight numbers can resemble airline numbering formats only when identified as demo data. Demo cancellation records must have their own synthetic source and fixed observation reference; none of the sources above supplies historical cancellation evidence for the product.

All added airports use the existing `Asia/Tehran` IANA-zone date boundary. Airport IATA codes and airline identifiers are separate namespaces: `TBZ`, for example, can identify Tabriz airport in the airport catalog and ATA Airlines in the ICAO airline catalog without equating those entities. Sepehran's ICAO identity is `SHI`, as listed by the FAA, not `SHN`.
